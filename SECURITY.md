# Security policy

Security and responsible data handling are core requirements for Kenzai Intelligence systems.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability or include sensitive customer, system, or credential information in GitHub.

Send a private report to [hello@kenzai.io](mailto:hello@kenzai.io) with:

- the affected repository, service, or URL;
- a concise description of the issue and its potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any suggested mitigation; and
- a safe way to contact you.

We will acknowledge the report, investigate it, and coordinate disclosure based on the severity and affected systems. Do not access, alter, retain, or disclose data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue safely.
